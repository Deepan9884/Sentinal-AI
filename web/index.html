MITRE EMB3D: EMB3D-T-0042 · CAPEC: CAPEC-624 · CWE: CWE-306 · Inherent Risk Score: 8.2 / 10.0 (Critical)
The external cellular MQTT broker operates without mutual TLS client certificate verification (TCP Port 1883). An unauthenticated remote actor can inject spoofed deceleration command payloads. The Telematics Control Unit translates ingress packets into SOME/IP format and relays them across Ethernet to the Central Gateway, which forwards unauthorized deceleration commands directly onto the ASIL-D safety CAN-FD bus.
